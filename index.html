YOUR LUNCH, YOUR CALL

Good choices
come in layers.

You do not need to decide everything at once. Work through your order one choice at a time.

01

Choose your format.

Think about how you want to eat. A sandwich, wrap, salad or protein bowl changes the base of the meal. Compare the actual item rather than assuming every format includes identical portions.

02

Find your main filling.

Start with the ingredient you want most. Chicken, steak, deli fillings and vegetables offer different directions. Read the complete item description before adding your personal changes.

03

Finish with the details.

Review vegetables, cheese, sauces and optional extras. Keep track of premium additions and check your completed selection so the final sandwich matches what you had in mind.

A SMARTER MENU CHECK

Before you
call it lunch.

A few small checks make it easier to compare your options.

Compare like with like.

A 6-inch sandwich and a Footlong are different orders. Match the size and extras when looking at prices, and include your drink or side in the final budget.

Look at the complete recipe.

Changes to bread, sauce and cheese can alter nutrition and ingredients. For dietary needs, use the information for the exact item you plan to buy.

Check the restaurant first.

Choose the right location before reviewing availability or promotions. Deals and menu choices can differ, so read the conditions attached to your selection.

QUICK ANSWERS

A few things worth knowing.

Are these prices the same at every Subway?

No. These examples help you compare starting points. Your restaurant and chosen ingredients determine the price for your order.

Can I customize my sandwich?

Check the available choices for your item and restaurant. Review bread, vegetables, cheese, sauce and any paid extras before completing the order.

Does this guide place food orders?

This is an independent menu guide. It helps you explore choices and does not process restaurant orders or payments.

How should I compare nutrition?

Use the nutrition information for the exact size and recipe you choose. Custom ingredients can change the finished item.

What is the difference between a sandwich, wrap and bowl?

The format changes the base and may change the portion, ingredients and price. Use the description for the exact item instead of assuming every format has the same recipe or serving size.

Does a sandwich price include a drink and side?

The sandwich prices in this guide refer to the listed sandwich alone. Meal offers have separate conditions. Check which drink and side are included before comparing a meal with individual items.

What catering choices can I compare here?

The catering table lists sandwich and wrap platters, individual boxed meals and cookie options. Check the serving size and available fillings for your restaurant before choosing quantities.

Are breakfast items available throughout the day?

Do not assume breakfast runs for the same hours at every restaurant. Select your location and pickup time, then check which breakfast items are offered for that order.
